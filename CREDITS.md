# Credits and sources

Sonic the Hedgehog 2 and its game assets belong to Sega and their respective creators. The assembly foundation is the [Sonic Retro Sonic 2 disassembly](https://github.com/sonicretro/s2disasm). Technical guides are available in the [Sonic Retro How-To collection](https://info.sonicretro.org/SCHG_How-to:Guide); the README lists the tutorials applied to this project.

Modifications are maintained by oranguthang. Included third-party tools retain their own license files and attribution.

## MD Debugger

MD Debugger and ConvSym are by [Vladikcomper](https://github.com/vladikcomper/md-modules). The included MIT license is in `_debug/md-debugger/LICENSE`.

## Flamedriver

Flamedriver, the Sonic 3 & Knuckles sound driver disassembled by MarkeyJester, with routines by Linncaki and improved by [Flamewing](https://github.com/flamewing/flamedriver), and its DAC samples are used under the BSD Zero Clause License in `sound/flamedriver/LICENSE`. Its SMPS2ASM include converts the Sonic 2 songs for it; `FeatureImprovedMusic` takes its note fixes from Flamedriver's `s2/music-improved` songs.

## Knuckles

Knuckles' code, art, mappings, animations and palettes come from the [Knuckles in Sonic 2 disassembly](https://github.com/sonicretro/s2disasm/tree/knuckles-in-sonic-2) (s2disasm's `knuckles-in-sonic-2` branch); the original game is by Sega and Sonic Team. The [Port Knuckles into Sonic 2](https://info.sonicretro.org/SCHG_How-to:Port_Knuckles_into_Sonic_2) guide by MainMemory and Painto was used as a reference.
